1. Purpose of Facebook
“Facebook makes it really easy to stay in touch with people.”
“It used to be the case that if you switched jobs, you might lose touch… now you don’t have to.”

👉 Key idea: Facebook solves the friction of maintaining relationships over time.

2. How people use Facebook
“People use it to share personal news, exchange photos, and stay connected.”
“It’s a way to find people you haven’t talked to in years.”

👉 Key idea: Social connection and rediscovery are core use cases.

3. Privacy control
“You can ignore friend requests.”
“Privacy settings allow you to control who sees your information.”

👉 Key idea: Users should control their visibility and connections.

4. On his age and leadership
“It’s not that big.”
“There are definitely elements of experience someone my age wouldn’t have.”
“There are also things that I can do that other people wouldn’t necessarily be able to.”

👉 Key idea: Youth is both a limitation and an advantage.

5. Lifestyle and mindset
“I’m not buying really expensive clothes.”
“I have a one-bedroom apartment with a mattress on the floor.”

👉 Key idea: Focus is on building, not wealth or status.

6. On innovation and growth
“About half my time is spent on business operations.”
Expanding from colleges → high schools → everyone.

👉 Key idea: Growth comes from systematically expanding access and use cases.

7. Platform strategy
“We’re letting developers build applications and keep the profits.”
“New programs emerge daily.”

👉 Key idea: Facebook grows by becoming a platform, not just a product.

8. Impact on communication
“Facebook is changing the way we communicate.”
“People can communicate with tens of thousands of others at the same time.”

👉 Key idea: Communication is becoming more scalable and network-driven.

9. Politics & influence
“It’s pretty effective for campaigning.”
“Politicians can communicate with large audiences directly.”

👉 Key idea: Facebook transforms political outreach and engagement.

10. On competition (Google, search)
“People can ask their friends instead of searching.”

👉 Key idea: Social recommendations can compete with traditional search.

11. On monetization
“We have to make money.”
“We have to support the company and make a profit.”

👉 Key idea: Revenue is necessary, even if the model is evolving.

12. Advertising philosophy
“Would you rather see a banner ad or something your friend actually likes?”
“When you share something, that’s effectively an endorsement.”

👉 Key idea: Ads should feel like social recommendations, not interruptions.

13. On privacy & mistakes (Beacon)
“It might take some work for us to get this exactly right.”
“This is something we think will be a good thing.”

👉 Key idea: Balancing monetization and privacy is difficult—and requires iteration.

14. On lawsuits & controversy
“We know we didn’t steal any ideas or code.”
“I don’t spend a lot of time worrying about it.”

👉 Key idea: Stay focused on building, not distractions.

15. On going public
“When going public makes sense, we’ll do that.”
“It’s highly unlikely we’ll go public in 2008.”

👉 Key idea: Timing matters—don’t rush major decisions.

🧠 One-Line Summary

Zuckerberg’s core thesis (early Facebook era):

Build a platform that makes staying connected effortless, grow it rapidly by opening it up, and monetize it through socially relevant interactions—while figuring out privacy and scale along the way.
