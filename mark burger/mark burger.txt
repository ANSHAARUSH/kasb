🕶️ The Future of Computing: AR Glasses
“This is probably going to be the next major platform after phones.”
AR glasses represent a shift toward more natural, social, and immersive computing.
“The trend in computing is it gets more ubiquitous, more natural, and more social.”
Full holographic AR could transform work, education, gaming, and communication.
“It’s going to remake so many different fields.”
🧩 Multiple Device Ecosystems (Not One Winner)
Future hardware will exist in layers:
Display-less AI glasses
Heads-up display glasses
Full holographic AR glasses
Mixed reality headsets
“All of those are going to be important long-term product lines.”
Different devices will serve different price points and use cases.
👥 Presence: The Core Goal of XR
“The main value we’re trying to bring is this feeling of presence.”
True innovation is making people feel physically together, not just connected.
“The holy grail… is delivering a deep sense of social presence.”
Presence is fragile:
It’s not about one feature working
It’s about nothing breaking the illusion
🤖 Personalized AI as a Second Pillar
“The other big track is personalized AI.”
AI becomes powerful when it understands:
Your life context
Your environment in real time
“Glasses are the ideal form factor… they can see what you see and hear what you hear.”
Future = deeply personal, context-aware intelligence
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 The Loneliness Problem (and Opportunity)
“The average person would like to have 10 friends and they have two.”
Digital tools are not replacing real connection, but filling unmet demand.
“People already don’t have as much connection as they would like.”
AR/AI could increase total connection, not reduce it.
⚖️ AI and Human Growth (Struggle vs Ease)
Some struggles should be removed (e.g. translation).
Some struggles are essential (e.g. thinking, learning).
“We’ll always find new things to struggle with.”
AI shifts effort from execution → creativity and iteration.
🧑‍💻 Learning in an AI World
“There are some things you need to be able to do yourself.”
Skills like coding:
Not just practical
But a way of thinking rigorously
“Teaching someone how to code… makes you a better thinker.”
🌐 Social Media + AI Evolution
Shift already happening:
From friends → creators/content
AI will:
Enhance content creation
Auto-generate personalized content

“AI is going to change almost every feature of every application.”
😰 Fear of Change vs Opportunity
“The pace of change is always a concerning thing.”

Raise quality standards
Increase competition

“Maintain curiosity”
Adapt instead of resisting
🔓 Open Source vs Closed AI
Two visions:
One centralized AI system
Many decentralized AIs
“I think we’re going to have a lot of different AI systems.”
On safety:
“Open source software is often more secure because more people can scrutinize it.”
Open ecosystems → innovation + resilience
🚀 The Big Unknown: AI Scaling
“We haven’t found the end yet.”
Key question:
Does AI keep improving with more data + compute?
“It’s possible we hit a limit… but it’s also possible we keep scaling for a long time.”
This decision drives hundreds of billions in investment
🔥 Compressed Philosophy (Zuckerberg’s Lens)

If you boil it down, his worldview is:

Build technology that increases human presence
Create AI that understands you personally
Expand connection, not replace it
Bet on long-term scaling of AI
Favor open ecosystems over centralized control
And above all:
“People who do well are generally curious… and try to use technology to live better lives.”
