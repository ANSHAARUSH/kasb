1. Build quickly and launch early
“I built the first version and launched it pretty quickly.”
“It spread because people wanted it.”

👉 Core idea: Speed + real demand > perfect planning.

2. Follow user demand to grow
“We didn’t start with a grand plan—we expanded because people kept asking for it.”

👉 Core idea: Let users pull the product forward.

3. Learn what you need, when you need it
“You can pick up new technologies very quickly if you need them.”
“Most of what we learned, we figured out along the way.”

👉 Core idea: Just-in-time learning beats over-preparation.

4. Hire for intelligence, not just skills
“We look for people who are really smart and can learn quickly.”
“Skills can be taught—how people think matters more.”

👉 Core idea: Adaptability > static expertise.

5. Focus on building, not bureaucracy
“It’s better to build things and fix them than to overthink everything upfront.”

👉 Core idea: Action creates momentum.

6. Simple ideas can be powerful
“The idea was just to make it easy to look people up.”
“That’s something people naturally want to do.”

👉 Core idea: Great products tap into basic human behavior.

7. Design for scale early
“We structured things so they could grow without breaking.”
“Breaking problems into smaller pieces made scaling manageable.”

👉 Core idea: Architecture decisions matter early.

8. Iterate instead of waiting for perfection
“You can always improve things later.”
“Getting something working is more important than getting it perfect.”

👉 Core idea: Progress over perfection.

9. Technology amplifies individuals
“A small group of people can build something used by millions.”

👉 Core idea: Leverage is everything in tech.

10. Ignore competitors, focus on execution
“We focused on what we were building instead of worrying about others.”

👉 Core idea: Execution wins more than strategy debates.

11. Long-term thinking matters
“We care more about building something valuable over time than short-term gains.”

👉 Core idea: Optimize for durability, not quick wins.

12. Give users control
“People should control what they share and who sees it.”

👉 Core idea: Trust is built through user control.

13. Social design solves technical problems
“When people use real identities, behavior improves naturally.”

👉 Core idea: Product design can shape behavior better than rules.

14. Meritocracy drives progress
“The people who build the best things have the most influence.”

👉 Core idea: Reward impact, not hierarchy.

15. Solve problems as they appear
“We didn’t have everything figured out—we just kept solving the next problem.”

👉 Core idea: Success is iterative problem-solving.

16. Great companies start with simple goals
“We weren’t trying to build a company—we were trying to build something useful.”

👉 Core idea: Utility first, business later.

🧠 One-Line Summary

Build something simple that people naturally want, launch fast, learn as you go, and scale it by focusing on smart people, strong fundamentals, and continuous iteration.
