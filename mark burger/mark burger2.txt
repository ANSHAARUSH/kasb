🗣️ Foundational Belief: Free Expression
“You only start one of these companies if you believe in giving people a voice.”
“The whole point of social media is giving people the ability to share what they want.”
“Our original mission is to give people the power to share and make the world more open and connected.”
⚖️ Shift Toward Censorship (and Regret)
“It was really in the last 10 years that people started pushing for ideological-based censorship.”
Two major turning points:
2016 (Trump election + Brexit)
2020 (COVID)
“We faced massive institutional pressure to start censoring content on ideological grounds.”
“Looking back, I gave too much deference to the media.”
🧪 The ‘Slippery Slope’ of Fact-Checking
“I was worried about becoming the decider of what is true in the world.”
Initial goal:
Only filter clear hoaxes
Reality:
“It just veered from there.”
“People felt fact-checkers were biased… especially in what they chose to check.”
“It’s a slippery slope… it destroyed a lot of trust.”
🦠 COVID & Government Pressure
“At the beginning, it made sense to defer to health authorities.”
Over time:
“It became very difficult to follow… everything was shifting.”
On pressure from government:
“They pushed us super hard to take down things that were true.”
“We’re not going to take down things that are true. That’s ridiculous.”
“They wanted us to take down humor and satire… we said no.”
🏛️ Government vs Platforms
“The First Amendment applies to the government, not companies.”
“Government officials pressuring companies to censor true things is pretty bad.”
“The U.S. government should be defending its companies, not attacking them.”
“When the U.S. government attacks its tech companies, it’s open season globally.”
🌍 Global Impact & Trust Collapse
“There’s been a broad decrease in trust… not just in the U.S., but globally.”
“People don’t trust mainstream institutions like they used to.”
“Social media is increasingly how people get information.”
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Rise of New “Cultural Elites”
“People don’t trust platforms—they trust creators.”
“There’s going to be a whole new class of creators people look to.”
“People want voices who give it to them straight.”
🧮 Moderation at Scale = Trade-offs
“3.2 billion people use one of our services every day.”
Core problem:
Precision vs Coverage
“If you lower confidence thresholds, you catch more bad content… but remove more innocent content.”
“At scale, even a small error rate means millions of mistakes.”
“Removing someone’s account by mistake is a terrible experience.”
⚙️ Policy Direction (New Approach)
Shift toward:
Higher precision (fewer wrongful removals)
“We’d rather remove less bad content than mistakenly punish innocent users.”
Replacing centralized fact-checking:
“Community-driven systems are better.”
“Show more context, not less content.”
🤖 On Bots & Manipulation
“Nation states create networks of fake accounts to manipulate perception.”
Detection strategy:
Behavioral patterns (not human-like activity)
“This is an adversarial space… they evolve, we evolve.”
