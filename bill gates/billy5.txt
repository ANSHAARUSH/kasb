1. Hire Extremely Driven, Passionate People
“We want the very brightest people we can find.”
“People who really wrap their mind around what they’re doing.”
“It lives with them all the time.”

👉 Core idea: Great companies are built by deeply obsessed, high-caliber individuals.

2. Work Ethic Matters More Than Hours
“People often work at night and much more than 8 hours a day.”

👉 Core idea: Intensity and commitment—not fixed schedules—drive innovation.

3. Programming is a Creative Craft
“It’s like writing a novel—except even better.”
“You create something that solves real problems.”

👉 Core idea: Software development is both creative and impactful.

4. Passion Over Money
“I don’t think anyone at the company is in it for the money.”
“It’s a much more exciting field than measuring how much we’re worth.”
“You can see people using what you build.”

👉 Core idea: Meaningful work and impact matter more than financial gain.

5. Speed and Timing Are Critical
“Things move very quickly.”
“The urgency to be the first is what caused me to drop out.”

👉 Core idea: In fast-moving industries, speed beats perfection.

6. Entrepreneurship Requires Acting Early
“We wanted to be the first to put BASIC on the microcomputer.”

👉 Core idea: First-mover advantage can define entire industries.

7. Leadership is About Energy and Clarity
“I enjoy getting people excited about what we’re going to get done.”
“Making sure ideas are measured properly.”
“Leading the company—that’s exciting.”

👉 Core idea: Leadership = vision + energy + structured execution.

8. Variety Prevents Burnout
“We’re not doing the same thing all day.”
“There’s always new things going on.”
“I don’t think it will ever be boring.”

👉 Core idea: Constant novelty and challenge sustain long-term motivation.

9. Customer Connection is Essential
“We go out and see end users.”
“We talk to customers.”

👉 Core idea: Building great products requires staying close to users.

10. Entrepreneurial Mindset Prefers Autonomy
“I’ve never really thought about working for someone else.”
“Answering to a boss would be a tough transition.”

👉 Core idea: True builders value independence and control over execution.

11. Business Success = Combining Tech + Execution
“I enjoy working with people and turning ideas into something real.”

👉 Core idea: Technical skill alone isn’t enough—you need execution and leadership.

12. Obsession Drives Mastery
“It lives with them all the time.”

👉 Core idea: Deep, continuous thinking about your work leads to excellence.

🔑 One-Line Summary
“Hire obsessed people, move fast, focus on impact—not money—and stay relentlessly engaged with the work.”
