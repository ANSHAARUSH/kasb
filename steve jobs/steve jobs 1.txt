Following curiosity and intuition can lead to unexpectedly valuable outcomes.
Trust is essential when the future looks uncertain.

“You can’t connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards.”
“You have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future.”
“Believing that the dots will connect… will give you the confidence to follow your heart.”
