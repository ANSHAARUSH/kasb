1. Marketing Is About Values, Not Features
In a noisy world, people won’t remember technical details.
Great brands communicate what they stand for, not what they sell.
Emotional connection matters more than specifications.

Key quotes:

“Marketing is about values.”
“This is a very complicated world… and we’re not going to get a chance to get people to remember much about us.”
“We have to be really clear on what we want them to know about us.”
“The way to do that is not to talk about speeds and feeds.”
2. Strong Brands Need Constant Care
Even the best brands lose relevance without attention.
Brand identity must be actively maintained and reinforced.

Key quote:

“Even a great brand needs investment and caring if it’s going to retain its relevance and vitality.”
3. Great Marketing Sells Emotion, Not Products
The best campaigns don’t focus on product features.
They represent ideals, identity, and inspiration.

Key examples (implied insight):

Nike sells emotion, not shoes.
“Got Milk?” succeeded without focusing on product benefits.

Key quote:

“They don’t ever talk about the products… they honor great athletes.”
4. Apple’s Core Belief
Apple is not just about making computers.
It stands for people who challenge the world and create change.

Key quotes:

“Apple at the core… believes that people with passion can change the world for the better.”
“What we’re about isn’t making boxes… Apple’s about something more than that.”
5. Values Don’t Change (Even When Everything Else Does)
Markets, products, and strategies evolve.
Core values must remain constant.

Key quote:

“Values and core values… shouldn’t change.”
6. Celebrate the Misfits & Innovators
Progress comes from unconventional thinkers.
The people who challenge norms are the ones who move humanity forward.

Key quotes:

“Here’s to the crazy ones. The misfits. The rebels. The troublemakers.”
“The ones who see things differently.”
“They push the human race forward.”
7. Think Different Philosophy
Innovation requires breaking rules and rejecting the status quo.
Belief in change is the starting point of transformation.

Key quotes:

“The people who are crazy enough to think they can change the world are the ones who do.”
“While some may see them as the crazy ones, we see genius.”
8. Brand = Identity + Purpose
A company must clearly answer:
Who are we?
What do we stand for?
Why do we exist?

Key quote:

“Our customers want to know: who is Apple and what is it that we stand for?”
Final Core Message
Focus on meaning, not mechanics.
Build identity, not just products.
Believe in people who challenge the world.

Signature line:

“Think different.”
